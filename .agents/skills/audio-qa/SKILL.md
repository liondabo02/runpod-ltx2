# Audio QA

Check every final language package.

- Character voice continuity
- Pronunciation and language correctness
- Dialogue timing against scene duration
- No clipping, broken files, silence gaps, or accidental overlaps
- Child-safe music and effects
- Music must not overpower dialogue
- Final release remains owner-gated
